      How the prediction works
      
    
      Woundex uses transparent clinical rules, not a black box. It grades infection with the IWGDF/IDSA classification, scores ulcer severity with SINBAD, and extends the line from the first to the latest wound area to project the reduction at week 4. Less than about 50% reduction at 4 weeks predicts that an ulcer will not heal by week 12.
      
  
      Safety & data
      
    
      • Every alert shows the signals that triggered it.
      • Decisions stay with the nurse and doctor; Woundex only prioritises and drafts.
      • All patients here are simulated. The page stores nothing and sends nothing.
